# WriteAway

**Keep typing or watch your words disappear.**

WriteAway is a focused writing app that helps you maintain momentum and overcome writer's block. The concept is simple: keep typing or your text starts to fade away, character by character.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Three Writing Modes:**
  - Normal: Visual warnings before deletion
  - Gentle: Text fades to gray first
  - Hardcore: Immediate deletion with minimal grace period

- **Customizable Sessions:**
  - Preset durations: 5, 10, 15, 30 minutes
  - Adjustable grace period (3-15 seconds)
  - Variable deletion speeds
  - **Continue Writing:** when the timer ends, keep your text on screen and start a new timer instead of facing a blank page

- **Writing-Focused Interface:**
  - Dark/light themes
  - Three text directions: left-to-right, right-to-left (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu) and vertical (Chinese, Japanese)
  - Prompt text shown in your browser's language for each direction
  - Fullscreen mode
  - Adjustable text size and fonts
  - Distraction-free writing environment

- **Multilingual Support:**
  - Chinese and Japanese counted by character
  - Works with pinyin and other input keyboards: deletion pauses while you pick a character

- **Session Statistics:**
  - Words this session and running total across continued sessions
  - Peak word count
  - Longest typing streak
  - Characters survived

- **Keyboard Shortcuts:**
  - ⌘/Ctrl + S: Save text
  - ⌘/Ctrl + F: Fullscreen
  - ⌘/Ctrl + D: Cycle text direction (→ ← ↓)

## 💡 Why WriteAway?

Based on the concept of "The Most Dangerous Writing App," WriteAway uses urgency to help you:
- Overcome perfectionism
- Get words on the page
- Build writing momentum
- Defeat writer's block
- Complete first drafts faster

Perfect for:
- Writers doing NaNoWriMo
- Students working on essays
- Anyone who overthinks their writing
- Creative writers seeking flow states

---

## 🎨 About

WriteAway was created to help writers get past the blank page and into a flow state. The pressure of disappearing text forces you to keep moving forward instead of endlessly editing.
